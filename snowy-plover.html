Snowy, Piping and Semipalmated Plovers

Snowy Plover is a western beach and salt-flat plover with 19 accepted Iowa records, most of them from late April through May. It’s small, pale and easy to pass off as a Piping Plover, or as a Semipalmated at a distance. Legs and bill sort most birds in seconds; the breast band and face back them up. Try the Snowy Plover Picker to work through a bird in the field.

Who to expect

  • Semipalmated PloverCommon migrantEarly April to late November, mostly May and August–September. Often in groups at the waterline.
  • Piping PloverRare migrantEndangered in Iowa. Migrants from early April to early October; a few nest along the Missouri River.
  • Snowy PloverAccidental19 accepted records, 1988–2022: most 24 Apr–30 May; also 14 Jun 1997, 27 Jun–9 Jul 2020, 22 Aug 2005 and 3 Sep 2008.

Behavior and pitfalls

  • Where.Semipalmateds feed at the waterline in groups with other shorebirds. Pipings are usually alone or in small groups. Snowy likes dry sand and alkaline flats; Iowa birds have turned up on reservoir beaches and drawn-down marshes.
  • Juvenile Piping.Its all-black bill looks like Snowy’s, but the bill is stubby and the legs are orange.
  • Faded or fall Piping.It shows only side patches and a mostly dark bill, much like Snowy. Check the legs, the bill shape and Snowy’s darker cheek.
  • Young Semipalmated.Dull legs and little orange in the bill. The dark back and complete band still give it away.
  • Great Plains Pipings.Iowa’s Pipings are mostly the darker interior subspecies, with thicker bands that are often complete. Don’t call one a Semipalmated on the band alone.
  • Wilson’s Plover.Bigger, with a long, heavy black bill and pinkish legs. There’s no Iowa record, but it’s the other “black-billed plover.”

Iowa timing

Extremes from the IOU early and late dates; peak windowscheck are general:

  • Semipalmated: spring from 1 Apr; fall until 28 Nov. Mostly May and August–September.
  • Piping: spring from 8 Apr; fall until 4 Oct.
  • Snowy: 15 of 19 records from 24 Apr to 30 May; others 14 Jun, 27 Jun–9 Jul, 22 Aug and 3 Sep.
