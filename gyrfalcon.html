Gyrfalcon and Its Look-alikes

Gyrfalcon, the biggest falcon, has 16 accepted Iowa records, all between late September and late March, mostly young birds in open farm country in the north and west. A big female Peregrine, a Prairie Falcon or a Goshawk can all pass for one at a glance. Wing shape, where the wingtips fall on a perched bird, the face and the wingpits do most of the work. Who to expect

  • Peregrine FalconBreeds; migrantAll year. About 16 pairs nested on buildings and Mississippi River bluffs in 2022; migrants pass through in spring and fall.
  • Prairie FalconRareLate August to May, mostly in the west.
  • American GoshawkRareWinter visitor, late August to April.
  • GyrfalconAccidental16 accepted records, 1992–2021: 28 Sep to 27 Mar, mostly November to March. The first was 27 Mar 1992 in Kossuth County.

Behavior and pitfalls

  • Hunting.Gyrs hunt low and chase prey horizontally, killing mostly on the ground; Iowa birds have taken jackrabbits and perched on power poles and treetops. Peregrines stoop from height.
  • Big female Peregrine.The largest females are as big as a male Gyr. Pale tundra Peregrines can look Gyr-like, but their wingtips reach the tail tip and the helmet is still bold.
  • Bluish cere and feet.They only mean a young falcon. Young Peregrines and Prairies have them too.
  • Gyr morphs.Gray is the usual morph in North America. White Gyrs are white with black spots; dark Gyrs look almost solid dark and can seem hooded.
  • Falconry hybrids and escapes.Gyr hybrids are hard or impossible to identify by plumage. A 2004 Toronto bird was called four different things before a leg band and tameness showed it was an escaped Gyr × Barbary. Look for bands and jesses, and note behavior. Iowa’s 2007 Boone County Gyr was accepted after a debate over where it came from.

Iowa timing

  • Gyrfalcon: accepted records 28 Sep to 27 Mar; late October and November birds at the Hitchcock hawkwatch.
  • Prairie Falcon: fall from 23 Aug; spring until 28 May.
  • American Goshawk: fall from 28 Aug; spring until 21 Apr.
  • Peregrine: all year.
