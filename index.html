Tricky Bird IDs

Decoders and references for birds that are easy to get wrong.

Decoders

Tap what you see and get a ranked list of what it could be.

  • White-headed Gull Picker White-headed gulls: pick the age, tap parts of the bird, choose what you see.
  • Small Gull Picker Small and hooded gulls: pick the age and season, then tap the wing, head, bill and tail.
  • Dowitcher Picker Short-billed or Long-billed: pick the plumage (or “not sure”), then call, date, tertials, scapulars and more.
  • Junco Picker Dark-eyed Junco forms: tap the hood, flanks, back, wingbars and tail.
  • Fall Warbler Picker Bay-breasted, Blackpoll or Pine: tap the feet, back, tail, flanks and face.
  • Oriole Picker All the orioles of the contiguous US: pick adult male, dull with a bib, or dull with no black, then tap the belly, face, back and wings.
