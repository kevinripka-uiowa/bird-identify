Yellow-billed and Black-billed Cuckoos

The bill settles it, but a cuckoo slipping between trees rarely gives you a look at it. Two marks work without the bill: a rufous flash in the outer wing (Yellow-billed), and the pattern under the tail. Both nest in Iowa. Try the Cuckoo Picker to work through a bird in the field.

Who to expect

  • Yellow-billed CuckooRegular; nestsStatewide in woodland edges, thickets and riparian woods; the commoner of the twocheck. Mostly May to September. Iowa’s earliest records are 24–28 April and the latest 30 October to 4 November.
  • Black-billed CuckooRegular; nestsStatewide but scarcercheck, in woodland edges and thickets. Mostly May to September. Iowa’s earliest records are 23 April to 1 May and the latest 17–25 October.

Step 1: Without the bill

These work on a bird flying past, at any age. Tap a check to see where to look.

These two work on any cuckoo flying past; the wing is the stronger mark.

Listen too. Soft, even “cu-cu-cu” groups of 2–5 notes on one pitch: Black-billed. A long, hollow, wooden knocking that slows and ends in “kowlp” notes: usually Yellow-billed. The calls overlap, so it’s fine to leave a heard-only bird as “cuckoo sp.”check

Step 2: Age it, then confirm

Adults

Both are slim, long-tailed, brown above and white below, about the size of a Blue Jay. A perched adult that shows its face is easy.

Pitfalls

  • Shade and backlight.Against a bright sky the undertail can look darker than it is, and a rufous wing can look plain. Wait for the bird to turn.
  • A heard bird isn’t always nameable.Black-billed’s long call has the same “kowlp” notes as Yellow-billed’s song.

Juveniles (July to October)

Young birds are duller, mostly silent, and their bills and eye-rings mislead. The wing is the best mark.

Pitfalls

  • A dark bill doesn’t make it a Black-billed.Young Yellow-billeds have all-dark bills for their first two months or so.
  • A pale eye-ring doesn’t make it a Yellow-billed.Young Black-billeds have buff or yellowish rings, not red.
  • Small tail spots.Young Yellow-billeds have smaller spots than adults; check the wing before calling it.

Iowa timing

From the Iowa Ornithologists’ Union early and late dates (the three most extreme records for each):

  • Yellow-billed: earliest 24 Apr 2016, 28 Apr 2001, 1 May 2018; latest 30 Oct 1999, 1 Nov 2008, 4 Nov 1990.
  • Black-billed: earliest 23 Apr 1989, 1 May 2010 (twice); latest 17 Oct 1986, 18 Oct 1986, 25 Oct 1975.
  • In practice: both arrive in early to mid-May and are quiet by September. A cuckoo after 25 October has so far always been a Yellow-billed.
