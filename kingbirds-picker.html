Kingbird Picker

For a yellow-bellied kingbird in south Texas. First rule out Western and Cassin’s: their chests are gray, not yellow, and their tails are black and square. Then pick the season, tap any part of the bird and choose what you see. Voice settles it; without voice, add up the bill, the wing, the chest and the tail, since each one overlaps.
